Jarvis

Jarvis is a personal assistant used by one person: its owner. It is not a public service and nobody else can sign in to it.

With the owner's permission it reads the owner's email (read-only) and calendar, finds things like exam dates, assignment deadlines and work shifts, adds them to a calendar it created for itself, and sends the owner a short morning summary.

It never sends email, and it never acts on anyone's behalf without the owner approving each action.
