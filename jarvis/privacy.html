Jarvis privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

Jarvis is a personal app used only by its owner. This policy covers the Google data it accesses.

What it accesses

  • Gmail, read-only: to find dates such as exams, deadlines and work shifts.
  • Google Calendar: to read the owner's events, and to create and update events on a separate calendar that Jarvis itself created.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to give the owner reminders and summaries.
  • It is stored on the owner's own server. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Short extracts of selected emails may be sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic) solely to pull out dates and deadlines. They are not used for any other purpose.

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
